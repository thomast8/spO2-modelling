7. CHOICES THE CONTRACT LEAVES TO THE HARNESS
---------------------------------------------

  - Delay prior: data-informed (mu0 = mean of all session means, beta0 = pooled between-session
    variance with alpha0 = 2, kappa0 = 2). LOSO re-chooses it without the held-out session.
  - Time zone for §9 time of day: Europe/London (the day-only STAmina imports start at 23:00Z in
    August).
  - Hold-volume standardisation: population SD over the labelled rows; band grid 0.05.
  - Hold model σ² prior: inverse-gamma a0 = 3, b0 = 0.045 (prior mean σ = 0.15), judgement.
  - Half-life: admitted like any input (must beat none on log score with coverage 70-90 %).
  - Coefficients for a candidate input: ridge on uncensored rows within volumes, prior sd 0.2 per input
    sd; censored rows do not inform coefficients.
  - level rowsToReach (the contract gives pb's only): smallest m with t90(2an+m)·sqrt(1/(1/s0² + (n+m)/σ²))
    ≤ (1 − ρ)·1.2816·s0.
  - A volume with no prior waits with 1 more max attempt at full lung (any outcome sets the placeholder).
  - Point baselines (PB-fallback, comparable-history) are scored with the base model's spread;
    comparable-history is openHoldEstimates for an open hold, the planned length for a timed one.
  - Static volumes: a measured TLC is the TLC that the FRC and RV ratios multiply; the ratio log-SDs
    are the larger sex's at 45 y.
  - Oxygen store σ_MC uses the reference diver with measured TLC and route-2 FRC/RV.
  - The delay calibration chain reuses the prior chosen on all sessions (mild leakage); it moves no floor.
  - Profile-field units carry the DiverProfile identifiers (sex, dateOfBirth, heightCm, weightKg); the
    sentence uses the display names. lungVolume.tlc is required only when every field is present and
    no TLC can be estimated; its display name is TLC.
  - Spirometry: unweighted (NHANES survey weights ignored); the 2011-12 cycle covers 84-89 % held out
    while the earlier two cover 90-93 %, a cycle effect that one σ scale cannot remove.

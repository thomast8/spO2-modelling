D. POST-HOLD SENSOR FIT (holds with at least 45 s of trace after the end)

  Latent SaO2: the last-30-s descent continues for T seconds past the end of the hold, sits on
  a floor for P seconds, then rises exponentially to the pre-hold baseline with a per-hold time
  constant tau_r. Sensor: first-order low-pass with time constant tau_s, started at the first
  observed value. T absorbs lung-to-finger transit and any pure device delay, and T + P is when
  the rise reaches the finger; neither splits into physiology and device here. tau_s and tau_r are
  both low-pass, so tau_s is profiled below; its interval is what the data allow, not what the
  fit reports.

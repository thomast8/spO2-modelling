v8.00 Results: v7.08 refit on the Nadir recordings (the baseline v8 has to beat)
================================================================================

Date: 2026-10-17
Scripts: backend/scripts/experiments/exp_v8_00/nadir_export.py      (loader + QC + inventory)
         backend/scripts/experiments/exp_v8_00/exp_v8_00_refit.py   (per-session v7.08 refit)
Outputs: backend/scripts/experiments/exp_v8_00/results/<group>.json and <group>.png

Nothing about the model changes in this experiment. The v7.08 Stage A and Stage B fit
functions are imported from exp_v7_08_ship.py and run, with the shipping configuration
and seed, on each session that has SpO2. The question is only: do v7's parameters hold
across sessions and devices, or was v7 a curve fit to 21 February 2026?

Loader decisions, all forced by v7's code rather than chosen:

  - v7's Stage B convolves the trace with the gamma kernel as an array, so it needs a
    complete 1 Hz grid. Dropouts and samples without skin contact are filled by linear
    interpolation. Every long gap in the September holds (11 to 21 s) sits in the first
    25 s of the hold on the 98-100 % plateau, where interpolation changes nothing. The
    one hold where the gap crosses the descent (18 September, 102 s without contact) is
    excluded.
  - Recovery follows v7's rule: up to 90 s after the hold, stopping once SpO2 is back at
    97 %. Nadir stores vitals per step, so the loader walks the steps after the hold.
  - QC is v7's: range < 5 % excluded; a nadir more than 30 s before the end of the hold
    excluded (v7 dropped RV#4 at 67 s for this reason and kept FRC#2 at 12 s). Two
    coverage rules were added for the Nadir data: more than 20 % of samples filled, or a
    gap longer than 10 s below 95 %, excludes the hold. v7 itself trained on FL#6 with a
    6 s gap near its nadir, so the line is above that.
  - Nadir `full` maps to v7 FL, `rv` to RV. There are no FRC and no packed holds.

Hold windows differ slightly from v7's own: the CSV's Apnea interval for FRC#2 is 135 s
where the v7 database had 132, because the app's parser trimmed rows the loader keeps.
The February refit below is therefore a close reproduction of v7.08, not a bit-identical
one, and that is the point: the same code on the same session through a second loader.


2. THINGS THE DATA SAYS BEFORE ANY FITTING
------------------------------------------

  - The post-hold window is short. Nadir's recovery breathe step is 20 s, and after the
    last hold of a session the trace ends with it. On 4 and 5 September the max holds end
    with SpO2 still falling (76 % and 71 % at the last sample). The nadir of the longest
    holds is not in the data. The cooldown after the last hold that Nadir has since added
    fixes this for future sessions.
  - Lag is visibly different per hold on the same device: nadir 1 s after the end of
    2026-09-05-FL1, 10 s after FL2 the same evening, 29 s after 2026-09-02-FL1. v7's
    per-hold delta_h existed for this; the spread here (-16 to +20 s, see below) is far
    wider than February's (-8 to +7 s).
  - Readings hold flat for 10 to 20 s after the end of a hold, then step. The PM100 (if
    that is what recorded these) behaves differently from the MightySat around the nadir.
    This is the device-specific sensor model the handoff asks for, and it cannot be
    separated from physiology with one device per session and no provenance.
  - The 8 September session is a CO2 table (five 150 s holds). SpO2 barely moves in four
    of them and v7's QC drops them. CO2 tables are not desaturation data.
  - No session has FRC holds. v7's FRC bound was its load-bearing constraint; there is
    nothing here to test it against.

4. WHAT THE REFIT SHOWS
-----------------------

  1. The pipeline reproduces v7.08. On the February session the new loader gives tau_0
     17.68 (v7.08: 17.68), p 3.40 (3.39), deltas within 0.03 s of the published ones,
     gamma 1.85 (1.86), the same three bound hits and the same +17 s nadir miss on FRC#2.
     k_co2 lands at 0.151 instead of 0.139 and FL6's tau_washout at 408 instead of 377
     (RV3's at 52 instead of 49.5): the two moved together, which is the k_co2 vs washout
     trade-off the spec documented in its own profile. The likely cause is the window:
     the loader keeps 3 to 7 apnea seconds per hold that the app's parser trimmed (FRC2
     +3, RV3 +4, FRC5 +3, FL6 +7). An 8 % shift in k_co2 from a few seconds of window is
     itself a finding about k_co2. Everything below is therefore about the data, not the
     code.

  2. The sensor lag does not hold. tau_0 is 17.7 s in February and 6.6, 20.9, 21.7 and
     30.5 s across four September sessions on, presumably, one device. Per-hold deltas
     that sat within -8 to +7 s in February now hit the +20 s bound (4 September FL5,
     effective lag 51 s) or sit at +-15.9 s with the zero-sum prior holding them
     symmetric (2 September). Two holds are pinned at the 5 s floor. A 51 s "lag" is
     not circulation time; it is the sensor model absorbing a trace shape the latent
     template cannot make.

  3. k_co2 is not a constant of this diver. February 0.139 (0.151 here), then 0.020 at
     the lower bound (2 September), 0.061, 0.100, 0.089 mmHg/s: 1.2 to 9 mmHg/min. gamma
     moves with it (2.51, 1.99, 2.43, 1.57), the trade-off the v7 spec documented in its
     own k_co2 profile. The spec called k_co2 an effective parameter; this is what that
     looks like once there is a second session.

  4. Stage A is fragile at two holds per session. On 5 September the sensor stage put
     FL1 at S_min 97 %, v_up 2.8 %/s and lag at the floor (R2a -0.05) while Stage B fits
     the same hold at 0.94. On 2 September v_up is at its 3 %/s bound on both holds. Eight
     parameters against two holds with 5 to 31 s of recovery is not identified, and the
     nadir-timing penalty has nothing to grip when the nadir is not in the trace.

  5. Stage B still describes the apnea window, as a curve fit does. For holds of 125 s
     and more, apnea-window R2a is 0.87 to 0.99, with per-hold tau_washout and paco2_0
     free. Full-lung tau_washout is 212, 321 and 381 s on three September evenings
     against 408 s in February; RV is 47 to 50 s against 52 s. paco2_0 sits at its 50
     mmHg upper bound on two full-lung holds. Nothing here is a parameter you could carry
     into a hold you have not seen.

  6. The September traces show a device behaviour the model has no term for. After the
     hold ends the reading stays flat at its end-of-hold value for 10 to 20 s, then steps
     (RV1 and RV2 on 4 September: flat, then a 10 to 13 point drop; FL4: flat, then down
     14 points, then up). A gamma kernel smooths; it cannot hold and step. This is where
     the inflated lags in item 2 come from, and it is the per-device sensor model the
     handoff asks for. With no device recorded on the session it cannot be attributed to
     the oximeter rather than to the finger, the diver's movement or the app's sampling.

  7. v7 does not apply below about 120 s, as its spec says: the one 150 s CO2-table hold
     that passed QC fits at R2a 0.33 with p at its bound.


5. VERDICT
----------

v7.08 is confirmed as what its own spec feared: a curve fit with diagnostics, not a
model. Refitted session by session, every global parameter moves well outside the
spread v7 called "identified": sensor lag 6.6 to 30.5 s, k_co2 0.02 to 0.15 mmHg/s,
gamma 1.6 to 2.5. The apnea-window fit quality survives because the per-hold parameters
absorb the differences, which is the definition of not transferring.

The baseline v8 has to beat is therefore not "mean R2a 0.97 in sample". It is: fit the
globals on all sessions but one and report the held-out session's apnea-window RMSE and
nadir error. The per-session fits here are the numbers that baseline will be compared
against; the pooled fit, when it finishes, gives the single-parameter-set version.

What survives from v7 for v8: the Severinghaus ODC and alveolar gas set-up, the sensor /
physiology separation as a hypothesis to test inside one model rather than as two fits,
and the habit of writing the success criteria before the fit. What does not: the
per-hold free parameters, the lambda priors, the lag-as-gamma-kernel sensor model, and
any claim about k_co2.

No v8 modelling is started in this experiment, on purpose. Ten usable holds in four
sessions, two lung volumes, no FRC, no device provenance and no captured nadir on the
max holds do not support a hierarchical model, and fitting one would only reproduce
v7's mistake with more parameters.


6. DATA NEEDED BEFORE V8 WORK CAN START
---------------------------------------

Everything in the v8 direction that needs lung-volume contrast, a sensor model per device
or partial pooling across sessions is blocked on data that does not exist yet. In order:

  1. A fresh Nadir Export through today. The one on disk stops on 29 September.
  2. Which oximeter recorded each session. The export does not say. If the September
     sessions mixed devices, nothing above can be interpreted.
  3. FRC holds, or confirmation that FRC is not part of the training. v7's structure
     depends on FRC; v8 needs at least one lung-volume contrast besides full vs RV.
  4. Sessions recorded with the post-last-hold cooldown on, so the nadir is captured.
  5. Any measured lung volumes (FVC from Health, lab TLC/RV) and body mass, for the
     mechanistic model's inputs. Not needed for the baseline, needed before v8.

Nothing here changes the framing rule: whatever comes next is retrospective and
descriptive. It reconstructs what happened; it never says how long a hold can go.
